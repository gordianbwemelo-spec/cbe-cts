# CBE Curriculum Tracking System — User Guide

## Signing in
Open the system link (or http://localhost:3000 when run locally). Enter your email
and password. On your first sign-in you will be asked to set your own password.

## Roles at a glance
- **Curriculum Coordinator** — enters and updates curriculum records, manages the
  review workflow, attaches documents, generates notifications.
- **Quality Assurance Manager (QAM)** — same editing rights, plus settings and the
  audit log.
- **Director of Academics** — same as QAM; oversees status and reports.
- **Administrator** — all of the above plus user management.
- **Management** — view-only: dashboard, register and reports.

## Everyday tasks
- **See what needs action:** Dashboard → “Curricula requiring action”. Anything
  expired, or within the review lead time (default 8 months) of expiry, appears
  here with a recommended action.
- **Change the review lead time or the assessment date:** the two controls at the
  top of the Dashboard / Register / Reports. The lead time is also saved under
  Settings so it applies to notifications.
- **Add or update a curriculum:** Add / Update (or “Open → Edit” from the
  Register). Every save is stamped with your role and the date.
- **Track a review:** open a curriculum → Review workflow → set the stage,
  reviewer and start date.
- **Attach documents:** open a curriculum → Documents → upload the approval letter
  or stamped curriculum (PDF/Word/image).
- **Send reminders:** Notifications → choose channels and recipients → Generate
  notifications. Dashboard alerts and voice work immediately; email/SMS send when a
  gateway is configured (otherwise a preview is recorded).
- **Produce a report:** Reports → choose scope → Generate report → Print / PDF or
  Export CSV.
